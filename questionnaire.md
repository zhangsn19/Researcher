Completing this pre-screening questionnaire takes approximately 3 minutes. Qualified respondents will be invited to a 40–60 minute remote interview and will receive 100 RMB as compensation upon completion.

All responses are strictly confidential and analyzed in aggregate or under de-identified pseudonyms in accordance with standard academic IRB protocols.
