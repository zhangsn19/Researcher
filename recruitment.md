# Call For Participants

Are you a graduate student or early-career researcher using Generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) in your academic workflows? We want to hear from you!

## Eligibility Criteria

- You are a novice researcher (master's student with additional research projects than your thesis, PhD candidate, Postdoc, or other early-career researchers graduated within a few years).

- You have used LLM-based chatbots for academic research tasks (e.g., ideation, coding, data analysis, literature review, or manuscript drafting) within the past 6 months.

## What the Interview Involves

- A 30-60 minute remote, semi-structured one-to-one interview via Zoom or Tencent Meetings (Audio needed).

- The interview will explore your experiences, privacy perceptions, and mitigation strategies when doing research using platforms involving AI.

## Compensation

You will receive 100CNY or local equivalent as a token of appreciation for your valuable time and insights.

## Privacy and Confidentiality Assurance

We deeply understand the sensitivity of your research. All responses will be anonymized. To protect your research, no raw interview data or transcripts will be shared publicly. This interview is IRB-approved.
